ABOUT US

Seafood, as it should be.
Since 1985.

THE HOUSE SIGNATURE

Butterfly Shrimp

Forty years in the making. Jumbo shrimp split open and splayed, dressed with the Seagullسي جلسي جل house butter, garlic, and a whisper of lemon, broiled until the edges catch. Available across all seven houses and, for most of our guests, the reason they keep coming back.
