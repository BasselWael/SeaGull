ABOUT US

Seafood, as it should be.
Since 1985.

Seagull is Egypt's legacy seafood house four decades of a single, stubborn idea: bring the catch in fresh, cook it simply, serve it without apology.

What began on Alexandria's El Max shoreline in 1985 now stretches from the Mediterranean to the Nile to the North Coast. Seven restaurants and one boutique hotel, each answering to the same kitchen.

The address changes. The standard does not.

OUR STORY

A journey down the coast,
and inland.

Seven chapters. One kitchen.

| 1985

El Max • Alexandria

Where it began. A seafood house on Alexandria's western shore, built on a single promise: fish straight from the boat, cooked over charcoal, served without ceremony.

THE ORIGINAL

| 1993

Marina • North Coast

A summer-only house on the Mediterranean. Three months a year, the doors open — and a generation of North Coast diners learned what a Seagull sunset should taste like.

SEASONAL · SUMMER ONLY

| 2002

Dokki • Cairo

Seagull moored in the capital for the first time on the Nile, opposite Cairo Tower. The sea came to the river.

ON THE NILE

| 2015

Gleem • Alexandria

Home waters. Perched above Gleem Beach, steps from the Mediterranean a return to where the story started.

| 2022

New Cairo • Tagamoa

A refined, modern house for the east side of the city. A new chapter and the same kitchen philosophy.

| 2024

Madinaty

A Seagull house inside All Seasons Mall, bringing the coast closer to Cairo's eastern communities.

| 2025

Sheikh Zayed

The latest chapter. At Centrada Mall on Cairo's west side the brand's newest address.

THE HOUSE SIGNATURE

Butterfly Shrimp

Forty years in the making. Jumbo shrimp split open and splayed, dressed with the Seagull house butter, garlic, and a whisper of lemon, broiled until the edges catch. Available across all seven houses and, for most of our guests, the reason they keep coming back.
