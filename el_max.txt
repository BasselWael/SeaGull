a single, stubborn idea: bring the catch in fresh, cook it
simply, serve it without apology.
What began on Alexandria's El Max shoreline in 1985
now stretches from the Mediterranean to the Nile to
the North Coast. Seven restaurants and one boutique
hotel, each answering to the same kitchen.
