About Us

Seafood, as it should be.

Since 1985.

eagull is Egypt's legacy seafood house four decades of
a single, stubborn idea: bring the catch in fresh, cook it
simply, serve it without apology.
What began on Alexandria's El Max shoreline in 1985
now stretches from the Mediterranean to the Nile to
the North Coast. Seven restaurants and one boutique
hotel, each answering to the same kitchen.
The address changes. The standard does not.

Where it began. A seafood house on Alexandria's
western shore, built on a single promise: fish
straight from the boat, cooked over charcoal,
served without ceremony.

A summer-only house on the Mediterranean.
Three months a year, the doors open — and a
generation of North Coast diners learned
what a Seagull sunset should taste like.
